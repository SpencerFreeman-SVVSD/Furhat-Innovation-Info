Innovation Center of St. Vrain Valley Schools (SVVSD)

Overview

The Innovation Center (IC) of St. Vrain Valley Schools is a cutting-edge educational facility located at 33 Quail Road, Longmont, CO 80504. Operating Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM, the center offers students experiential learning opportunities that transcend traditional classroom settings. Through advanced coursework, internships, capstone projects, and industry certifications, students are prepared to become future leaders and innovators.

Key Programs and Focus Areas

The IC provides a diverse array of programs across multiple disciplines:

Aeronautics: Students engage in engineering and flight training, with opportunities to earn FAA certifications.

Artificial Intelligence (AI): Courses and projects focus on AI applications and development.

Biosciences: Hands-on experiences in biomedical fields, including lab work and research.

Cybersecurity: Training in digital security practices and technologies.

Entrepreneurship: Programs that foster business development skills and innovation.

Robotics: Design and programming of robotic systems, including participation in competitions.

STEM Education: Integrated science, technology, engineering, and math curricula.

Video Arts & Virtual Reality: Creative media production and immersive technology experiences.

P-TEACH (Pathways to Teaching): Preparation for careers in education.

Work-Based Learning: Real-world job experiences with local industry partners.

Students can also join specialized project teams, such as the Conservation Data Science Team, Aero Team, and Socially Assistive Robotics (SARs) Team, to work on collaborative projects that address real-world challenges.

Summer Programs

The IC offers several summer programs designed to engage students in innovative learning experiences:

Innovation Academy: A program for students to explore global issues and develop problem-solving skills through themes like food systems and sustainable transportation.

Summer Camps: Hands-on STEAM activities for students entering grades 1–12, covering topics such as drone engineering and nature exploration.

Summer High School Classes: Courses offering 0.5 practical arts credits toward graduation, available to SVVSD students in grades 9–12.

Facilities and Resources

The 50,000-square-foot Innovation Center is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including:

A biomedical lab for advanced scientific research.

An entrepreneurial zone for student-led business development.

An aerospace lab featuring flight simulators.

A fabrication lab for prototyping and manufacturing.

Additionally, the ST. VRAINNOVATION Future-Ready Innovation Lab is a mobile unit that brings technology and innovation experiences, such as virtual reality and robotics, directly to students throughout the district.

Student Opportunities

The IC emphasizes real-world applications of learning by providing students with opportunities to:

Participate in paid internships and project-based learning with local technology companies.

Collaborate on projects that address global challenges, such as developing aquatic robotics to study endangered species.

Gain industry-recognized certifications and practical experience in their fields of interest.

Enrollment and Participation

Eligibility: Open to all SVVSD high school students (grades 9–12), including those from non-traditional schools and homeschoolers within the district.

Course Credits: Most classes offer 0.5 practical arts credits applicable toward graduation requirements.

Transportation: Free bus transportation is provided between home high schools and the IC.

Lunch Services: Free lunch is available to all students, with additional snacks and beverages for purchase.

What is the Innovation Center, and what do students do here?
The Innovation Center is a special learning space where students work on real-world projects in areas like robotics, aerospace, artificial intelligence, and more. They learn by doing—using tools, technology, and teamwork to solve problems and explore careers.

Who can participate in the programs at the Innovation Center?
Students from across the St. Vrain Valley School District in middle and high school can apply or enroll in programs. Some students come during the school day, and others join after school or over the summer.

How is this different from a regular school classroom?
Instead of only learning from books or lectures, students here get hands-on experience. They work with real tools, collaborate on projects, and even connect with professionals from industry. It’s like a mix of school, lab, and workplace.

What is quantum technology, and why is it important for students to learn about it?
Quantum technology is a new field that uses the science of very small particles—like atoms—to build powerful tools like quantum computers. It’s expected to change how we solve problems in medicine, cybersecurity, and more. Learning about it now helps students get ready for future jobs.

How will students learn about quantum topics here?
Students are introduced to basic ideas through lessons, hands-on activities, and special events. They use models, games, and software to explore how quantum systems work. Some also visit real quantum labs or meet scientists in the field.
